# E-Commerce Sales & Customer Analytics

An end-to-end business analytics project combining **Python, SQL, and Power BI** to analyze sales, customer behavior, product profitability, regional performance, discounting, and shipping operations.

## Project Overview

The objective of this project is to transform transactional e-commerce data into actionable business insights through data cleaning, exploratory analysis, SQL-based business queries, and an interactive Power BI dashboard.

### Business Questions

- How are sales and profit changing over time?
- Which customers contribute the most revenue and profit?
- Which products and sub-categories drive profitability?
- Which products generate high sales but negative profit?
- How does discounting relate to profitability?
- Which regions and states perform well or poorly?
- How does shipping performance vary across regions and shipping modes?
